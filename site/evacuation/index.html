MODEL & GEOMETRY CONTRACT

Two routes to the same accounting rule

Each floor is 100 × 100 m. Native motion follows one continuous unfolded 2D domain. The two display charts reflect the upper native coordinates. Stair travel has a 12 m native path and a declared 4 m rise; this is not a 3D biomechanics model.

Internal: upper occupants descend the shared stairs, merge with ground-floor traffic, and reach a ground final exit. Exterior: upper emergency routes use dedicated exterior stairs to grade-level outdoor zones; the shared stairs remain available for incoming visitors.

Upper doors are never counted as safety. Safety requires an observed native centre inside the outdoor zone, at least 0.6 m beyond its final discharge. The zones are demonstration destinations, not a fire-safe distance assessment.

Live source: random arrivals automatically stop after the configured finite window (30 s by default). Stop inflow early closes requests sooner at an acknowledged native tick. Already requested people remain counted and complete an upper-floor visit, then evacuate. Pausing playback never stops admissions. Saved comparisons use the same 30 s source-stop schedule, with a 600 s drain horizon.
